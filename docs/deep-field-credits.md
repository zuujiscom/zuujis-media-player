# Deep Field image credits

The Deep Field visualizer uses photographs from the NASA Image and Video Library
(https://images.nasa.gov). NASA imagery is generally not copyrighted; these are
Hubble Space Telescope images credited to NASA, ESA and STScI (see each record
for the full credit line). They were cropped, had the sky level subtracted and
were faded to black at the rim to make `DeepFieldGalaxies`; `DeepFieldSky` is
the deep-field image, resized. Use here does not imply endorsement by NASA.
